# Witch & Ward: core rules (one page, v0.9)

The chapters (`book/src/chapters/`) are now the full rules; this page is the approved one-page summary, and where they differ the chapters win. The numbers marked **[T]** are in the table at the bottom; the simulator set them (`sim/REPORT.md`). Every change is logged in `book/REVIEW.md`.

**The game.** 3–5 players and a Storyteller. Each player is a witch in **the coven**, or one player may be the coven's **guardian**. One session is one **episode**. Episodes can be strung into a **season**. Set every number at the start of the episode; they don't change if someone joins or leaves.

**Characters.** A witch has four ratings: **Heart** (people), **Wits** (noticing, research), **Nerve** (moving, fighting, standing firm) and **Craft** (spells, potions); put 3, 2, 2 and 1 into them. Each witch has one **Power** (roll a d6 in this order, or pick: Force, Flame, Sight, Voice, Veil, Wild; no two witches share one), a **Life** (a job or role; roll a d6, rerolling one someone has, or pick: 1 student, 2 nurse, 3 shop worker, 4 teacher, 5 mechanic, 6 bus driver), 4 **Hurt** boxes and 5 **Magic** each episode (Magic has no top limit). The guardian puts 3, 2 and 2 into Heart, Wits and Nerve, and has 5 Hurt boxes and 5 **Ward** each episode. Unspent Magic and Ward are lost at the end of the episode, so spend them, but keep 2 Magic: 1 to join the Unmaking and 1 for a die or a second try.

**The roll.** Roll dice equal to the rating. Each 4, 5 or 6 is a success. The Storyteller sets the **Difficulty** (usually 1–4; the Unmaking can be higher).
- **Success:** successes ≥ Difficulty. **Critical:** a success that shows two or more 6s. It does one thing extra: +1 damage, +1 Clue, +1 potion or −1 more Exposure, as fits; otherwise +1 die on your next roll this episode (inside the +2 limit).
- **Partial:** at least half the Difficulty (round up), but less than all of it. It works, with a cost. (At Difficulty 1 there is no Partial.)
- **Failure:** fewer than that. It doesn't work, and there is a cost.
- **Cost:** in a showdown, 1 Hurt. Otherwise, Exposure +1 if you were casting out in the city, or else 1 Hurt.
- **Casting** means using a Power, a spell, a potion or a guardian gift. A helper's die or Sense doesn't make your roll casting. Casting Hide never raises Exposure, so its cost is always 1 Hurt.
- **Extra dice:** your Power adds 2 dice when it fits (you may leave it off; then the roll isn't casting). On top of that, at most **+2 dice from everything else together**: helpers (1 Magic or Ward each; helping takes no action, so anyone can help, even after acting or while Down), your Life (+1 when the Storyteller agrees it fits), the Book (+1), or your own Magic (Ward for the guardian; 1 each). Sense's die doesn't count toward the limit.
- **Group check:** when everyone must do the same thing, everyone rolls; it isn't an action. One cost, taken by whoever rolled worst.

**Spells** (Craft, 1 Magic). **Reveal**: a lead using Craft; a success gives 1 more Clue. **Bind** (against Defence): the threat loses its next attack. **Blast**: an attack using Craft; 1 more damage if it works. **Shelter** (Difficulty 2): until the end of the threat's next turn, one person can't be hurt by its attacks and doesn't roll to defend (it ends if you go Down). **Hide** (Difficulty 2): Exposure −1. The **Unmaking** (below). Any other spell: the closest one, at +1 Difficulty if it's a stretch.
**Powers.** Each adds 2 dice when it fits. Once per scene its **Surge** (2 Magic, paid before you roll and spent even on a Failure, added to an action using the Power) does more, on a Partial or better; the whole showdown counts as one scene. **Force** (attacks, freeing): the threat loses its next attack. **Flame** (attacks): 2 more damage. **Sight** (leads, defending, studying): 1 more Clue; or, with no action, before the defence roll, one attack aimed at anyone misses (this isn't a lost attack). **Voice** (talking leads, Hide): 1 more Clue, or Exposure −1. **Veil** (defending, searching leads, Hide): no Exposure from casting for the rest of the scene, or in a showdown for this round and the next (a casting roll's cost becomes 1 Hurt). **Wild** (talking and searching leads, Bind, brewing): 1 more Clue or potion; or in a showdown, the threat's Defence is 1 lower for everyone this round. A **talking lead** uses Heart; a **searching lead** uses Wits.
**Potions** (brew at home: Craft against the Lead Difficulty; a success makes two, a Critical three, a Partial one with its cost). **Healing**: clear 2 Hurt. **Blasting**: use it with your attack; if the attack works, 2 more damage. **Binding**: use it with your attack; if the attack works, the threat loses its next attack. Say you're using a potion before you roll; it's used up either way. The coven shares its potions and can have at most one per character (the guardian counts if a player plays it); extra potions from a brew are lost. Using one takes no action: on your turn, on yourself or someone near you; each character uses at most one a round (or scene). They spoil at the end of the episode.

**The episode.**
1. **Opening.** The threat strikes. Doom, Exposure and Clues start at 0.
2. **Investigation**, in **scenes**. Each character takes one action: follow a **lead** out in the city (Heart, Wits or Reveal against the Lead Difficulty: a success gives 1 Clue, a Critical 2, a Partial 1 with a cost); research the **Book** at home (a lead using Wits, +1 die, with no Exposure; one character per scene, who may also make one brewing roll that scene; each roll has its own result and cost); brew a potion at home; rest (clear all Hurt); cover up out in the city (Hide); or answer their **Life** (no roll; gain 1 Magic). Each witch's Life calls once per episode, and they can answer it in any scene; if they haven't by the showdown, they lose 1 Magic (or take 1 Hurt if they have none). At the end of each scene, **Doom +1**. The Showdown mark is only checked then, even if Doom rose during the scene. Then, if the coven has half the Clues **[T]** (rounded up), it knows what the threat is and where, and may **go to it** (this takes no scene): the showdown is in a hidden place, and the innocent isn't there. With all the Clues it knows the **Weakness**. But if Doom has reached the **Showdown mark** (5), it's too late: the threat **comes to them**, and the showdown starts in a public place with the innocent **in its grip** (whatever the coven did for them before).
3. **Showdown**, in **rounds**. Each character takes one action, in any order (or does nothing): attack (Nerve, or Craft with Blast; a Power that fits adds 2 dice; against the threat's Defence: a success does 2 damage, a Critical 3, a Partial 1 with a cost; a Failure costs 1 Hurt), cast a spell, **free** the innocent, **guard** the innocent (until the end of the threat's next turn, if it goes for them it attacks you instead; it ends if you go Down), **study** the threat (Wits against Defence: 1 Clue, so the Weakness can be found mid-fight), or use a gift. Then **the threat acts** (in the first round it acts before the characters instead). In a public place, every round in which anyone was casting raises **Exposure by 1** (once per round). Then **Doom +1**.
4. **Closing.** Life scenes, consequences, carry-over.

**The innocent** (public showdowns only). The threat starts with them in its grip. To **free** them, roll Nerve against Defence; only a success frees them (a Partial doesn't, and still costs 1 Hurt). If they're still in its grip at the end of the first round, they're lost. While in its grip they can't be guarded or carried. Once free, if the threat takes them, they're lost. A Broken threat lets go.
**The Weakness.** Until the coven knows it, the threat's Defence is 1 higher. Once it knows it, attack rolls (including Blast) that work do 1 more damage; potions don't.
**The threat.** Stats [T]: **Defence** (to hurt it), **Danger** (to defend against it), **Hit**, **Toughness**. It makes 1 attack a round against 3 characters, 2 against 4 or 5. It loses at most one attack a round, whatever the cause; any more are wasted. Against a coven of 3 witches and no guardian player, it has 3 more Toughness. For each attack, roll a d6 (even for an attack Hold has aimed, since a 6 can still take the innocent): **1–3** it attacks whoever hurt it last (if nobody has, or they're Down, use 4–5); **4–5** whoever has the most Hurt marked and is still standing (ties: the Storyteller picks); **6** its signature move (if it has none, it attacks two different characters), but if the innocent is there and free it goes for them instead: it attacks their guard, or takes them if nobody is guarding them (taking them isn't an attack, so Hold can't stop it). The target rolls Nerve (plus a Power that fits) against Danger: Success no Hurt, Partial 1 Hurt, Failure Hit Hurt. When its Toughness is full, it is **Broken**: it makes no more rolls or moves, Doom stops rising, and it flees at the end of the next round.
**The Unmaking** (only against a Broken threat). One standing witch leads with Craft as their action and pays 1 Magic. Every other witch may join for 1 Magic, and each one adds **2 dice**; joiners don't count toward the +2 limit. A standing witch joins as their action, so must not have acted this round; a Down witch joins without one. Difficulty: **the number of witches in the coven**, +2 without the Weakness. Only a success destroys the threat. On a Partial or Failure it isn't destroyed and nobody takes Hurt: try again next round if it's still there.

**The guardian's gifts** (1 Ward each). **Mend** (action): clear 2 Hurt from someone it touches. **Shield** (no action, once a round): take an attack meant for someone near it (in a showdown, everyone is near). **Carry** (action): take one person to a place it knows, such as the freed innocent out of a fight. It can't carry the coven out of a showdown or past a threat. **Sense** (no action, once a scene): +1 die on someone else's lead this scene, outside the +2 limit. **Hold** (action, no Ward, not casting): Nerve against Defence; if it works, the threat's next attack must target the guardian. The guardian can free and guard the innocent, study the threat and research the Book like anyone, but can't cast spells, brew or use potions, join the Unmaking or damage a threat; it can help with Ward. A Down guardian fades back to the home for the rest of the scene (in a showdown, the rest of the fight) and can't help from there. **No guardian player:** once per episode, on any character's turn, the coven may call it for one Mend or one Carry; calling takes no action and no roll, and counts as casting.

**Tracks.** **Hurt:** full boxes = **Down**: no actions except resting during the investigation, and no potions on yourself (others can use one on you), but you can still help and join the Unmaking; back up when Hurt drops below full. Hurt can't go past full, and it clears between episodes (except a Scar). **Exposure:** when full [T], the coven is **Seen**; it stays full, and any further rise becomes **Doom +1** instead (once per round or scene). Hide can lower Exposure before then, never below 0. **Doom:** 12 boxes; nothing lowers it. Reaching the mark (5) during the investigation brings the threat; when full, the plan is complete: the innocent is lost and the threat escapes. Doom doesn't rise once the threat is Broken (not even from Seen). Extra Clues or damage past the total are lost. Round 1: the threat's attacks, the characters' actions, the innocent, Exposure, Doom. Later rounds: the characters' actions, the threat's attacks, Exposure, Doom.

**The end.** The episode ends at once (the round's later steps don't happen) when the threat is destroyed, flees, or Doom fills, when every witch is Down at the end of a showdown round, or when the coven **retreats** (at the start of any round; everyone leaves together).
- **Win:** destroyed, and the innocent safe. **Partial:** it fled, or it was destroyed but the innocent was lost. **Loss:** Doom filled, every witch Down, or retreat.
- **Seen** drops the result one step (a Loss stays a Loss).
- Nobody dies from the dice. A witch who is Down when the coven loses (except by retreating) gets a **Scar**: 1 Hurt at the start of the next episode.
- **Season:** destroyed threats get a page in the Book (next time, start with half the Clues). A threat that wasn't destroyed comes back later, when the Storyteller chooses, with 2 more Toughness each time. On a retreat, or when every witch is Down, an innocent still in the fight is lost. After every second episode, each witch raises one rating by 1 (most 4). After the 2nd and the 4th episodes, threats also get 2 more Toughness per witch (so 4 more per witch from the 5th episode on).

**[T] Numbers** (w = witches, n = witches plus the guardian, if a player plays it)

| | Easy | Standard | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead Difficulty | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Threat Defence / Danger | 1 / 1 | 2 / 3 | 2 / 3 |
| Hit (Hurt on a failed defence) | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Toughness | 3 × w | 3 × w | 4 × w |
| Clues needed | 3 × n | 4 × n − 1 | 4 × n |
| Exposure boxes | 5 | 4 | 4 |
