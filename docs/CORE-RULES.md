# Witch & Ward: core rules (one page, v0.3)

Until the chapters exist, this page is the rules. The numbers marked **[T]** are in the table at the bottom; the simulator set them (`sim/REPORT.md`). Every change is logged in `book/REVIEW.md`.

**The game.** 3–5 players and a Storyteller. Each player is a witch in **the coven**, or one player may be the coven's **guardian**. One session is one **episode**. Episodes can be strung into a **season**.

**Characters.** A witch has four ratings: **Heart** (people), **Wits** (noticing, research), **Nerve** (moving, fighting, standing firm) and **Craft** (spells, potions); put 3, 2, 2 and 1 into them. Each witch also has one **Power** (Force, Flame, Sight, Voice, Veil or Wild), a **Life** (a job or role), 4 **Hurt** boxes and 5 **Magic** each episode. The guardian has Heart, Wits and Nerve (3, 2, 2), 5 Hurt boxes and 5 **Ward** each episode. Unspent Magic and Ward are lost at the end of the episode, so spend them, but keep 2 Magic for the Vanquish.

**The roll.** Roll dice equal to the rating. Each 4, 5 or 6 is a success. The Storyteller sets the **Difficulty** (1–4).
- **Success:** successes ≥ Difficulty. **Critical:** a success that shows two or more 6s; it does one thing extra.
- **Partial:** at least half the Difficulty (round up), but less than all of it. It works, with a cost.
- **Failure:** fewer than that. It doesn't work, and there is a cost.
- **Cost:** in a showdown, 1 Hurt. Otherwise, Exposure +1 if you used magic out in the city, or else 1 Hurt.
- **Magic** means a Power, a spell, a potion or a guardian gift.
- **Extra dice:** your Power adds 2 dice when it fits. On top of that, at most **+2 dice from everything else together**: helpers (1 Magic or Ward each, no action needed), your Life (+1 when it fits), the Book (+1), Sense (+1), or your own Magic (1 each).
- **Group check:** when everyone must do the same thing, everyone rolls; the group takes one cost, from the worst result.

**Spells** (Craft, 1 Magic). **Reveal**: a lead using Craft; a success gives 1 more Clue. **Bind** (against Defence): the threat loses its next attack. **Blast**: an attack using Craft; 1 more damage if it works. **Shelter** (Difficulty 2): one person can't be hurt until your next action. **Hide** (Difficulty 2): Exposure −1 (−2 on a Critical). The **Vanquish** (below). Any other spell: the closest one, at +1 Difficulty if it's a stretch.
**Powers.** Each adds 2 dice when it fits. Once per scene its **Surge** (2 Magic, added to an action using the Power) does more, on a Partial or better; the whole showdown counts as one scene. **Force** (attacks, freeing): the threat loses its next attack. **Flame** (attacks): 2 more damage. **Sight** (leads, defending, studying): 1 more Clue; or, with no action, one attack aimed at anyone misses. **Voice** (talking leads, Hide): 1 more Clue, or Exposure −1. **Veil** (defending, searching leads, Hide): no Exposure from magic for the rest of the scene. **Wild** (talking and searching leads, Bind, brewing): the threat's Defence is 1 lower for everyone this round.
**Potions** (brew at home: Craft against the Lead Difficulty; a Critical makes two). **Healing**: clear 2 Hurt. **Blasting**: 2 damage, no roll. **Binding**: the threat loses its next attack, no roll. Using one is an action. They spoil at the end of the episode.

**The episode.**
1. **Opening.** The threat strikes. Doom, Exposure and Clues start at 0.
2. **Investigation**, in **scenes**. Each character takes one action: follow a **lead** out in the city (Heart, Wits or Reveal against the Lead Difficulty: a success gives 1 Clue, a Critical 2, a Partial 1 with a cost); research the **Book** at home (Wits +1 die; one character per scene); brew a potion at home; rest (clear all Hurt; the one action a Down character can take); cover up out in the city (Hide); or answer their **Life** (no roll; gain 1 Magic). Then **Doom +1**. Each witch's Life calls once per episode; if they haven't answered it by the showdown, they lose 1 Magic. With half the Clues **[T]** (rounded up), the coven knows what the threat is and where it is, and may **go to it**: the showdown is in a hidden place, and the innocent isn't there. With all the Clues it knows the **Weakness**. If Doom reaches the **Showdown mark** (5) first, the threat **comes to them**: the showdown starts at once, in a public place, with the threat **holding** the innocent.
3. **Showdown**, in **rounds**. Each character takes one action, in any order: attack (Nerve, Blast, or a Power that fits, against the threat's Defence: a success does 2 damage, a Critical 3, a Partial 1 with a cost; a Failure costs 1 Hurt), cast a spell, use a potion, **free** or **guard** the innocent, **study** the threat (Wits against Defence: 1 Clue, so the Weakness can be found mid-fight), or use a gift. Then **the threat acts**. Then **Doom +1**. In a public place, every round in which anyone used magic raises **Exposure by 1** (once per round).
4. **Closing.** Life scenes, consequences, carry-over.

**The innocent** (public showdowns only). The threat starts holding them. To **free** them, a character rolls Nerve against Defence; only a success frees them. If they're still held at the end of the first round, they're lost. While held, they can't be guarded or carried. A Broken threat lets go.
**The Weakness.** Until the coven knows it, the threat's Defence is 1 higher. Once it knows it, attacks that work do 1 more damage.
**The threat.** Stats [T]: Defence, Attack, Hit, Toughness. It makes 1 attack a round against 3 characters, 2 against 4 or 5. For each, roll a d6: **1–3** it attacks whoever hurt it last; **4–5** whoever is most hurt and still standing; **6** its signature move, but if the innocent is there and free it goes for them instead: it attacks their guard, or takes them if nobody is guarding them (taking them isn't an attack, so Hold can't stop it). The target rolls Nerve (plus a Power that fits) against Attack: Success no Hurt, Partial 1 Hurt, Failure Hit Hurt. When its Toughness is full, it is **Broken**: it stops attacking, Doom stops rising, and it flees at the end of the next round.
**The Vanquish** (only against a Broken threat). One witch leads with Craft and pays 1 Magic. Every other witch may join for 1 Magic (a standing witch uses their action; a Down witch doesn't), and each one adds **2 dice**; joiners don't count toward the +2 limit. Difficulty: **the number of witches in the coven**, +2 without the Weakness. Only a success destroys the threat. On a Partial or Failure it holds on, with no other cost: try again next round if it's still there. A Broken threat stays until the end of the next round, so the coven can wait for a round where everyone can join.

**The guardian's gifts** (1 Ward each). **Mend** (action): clear 2 Hurt from someone it touches. **Shield** (no action): take an attack meant for someone near it. **Carry** (action): take one person to a place it knows, such as the innocent out of a fight. It can't carry the coven out of a showdown or past a threat. **Sense** (no action): +1 die on a lead this scene. **Hold** (action, no Ward): Nerve against Defence; on a success the threat's next attack must target the guardian. The guardian can't cast spells, use potions, join the Vanquish or damage a threat. A Down guardian fades back to the home for the rest of the scene. **No guardian player:** once per episode, the coven may call it for one Mend or one Carry.

**Tracks.** **Hurt:** full boxes = **Down** (can't act, but can still help and join the Vanquish; back up when Hurt drops below full). Hurt can't go past full. **Exposure:** when full [T], the coven is **Seen**; it stays full for the rest of the episode. Hide can lower it before then, never below 0. **Doom:** 12 boxes. Reaching the mark (5) starts the showdown; when full, the threat's plan is complete and it escapes with the innocent. Doom doesn't rise once the threat is Broken. In a round, resolve the characters' actions, then the threat's attacks, then Exposure, then Doom.

**The end.** The episode ends when the threat is destroyed, flees, or Doom fills, when every witch is Down, or when the coven **retreats** (at the start of any round).
- **Win:** destroyed, and the innocent safe. **Partial:** it fled, or it was destroyed but the innocent was lost. **Loss:** Doom filled, every witch Down, or retreat.
- **Seen** drops the result one step (a Loss stays a Loss).
- Nobody dies from the dice. A witch who is Down when the coven loses gets a **Scar**: 1 Hurt at the start of the next episode.
- **Season:** destroyed threats get a page in the Book (next time, start knowing what it is). A threat that fled comes back later with 2 more Toughness. After every second episode, raise one rating by 1 (most 4).

**[T] Numbers** (w = witches, n = witches plus the guardian, if a player plays it)

| | Easy | Standard | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead Difficulty | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Threat Defence / Attack | 1 / 1 | 2 / 2 | 2 / 2 |
| Hit (Hurt on a failed defence) | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Toughness | 3 × w | 3 × w | 4 × w |
| Clues needed | 3 × n | 4 × n − 1 | 4 × n |
| Exposure boxes | 5 | 4 | 4 |
