# Witch & Ward: ready-made content (v0.9 rules)

The names, places, threats and episodes the rulebook and the Foundry system share. Chosen under Richard's standing rule (pick the recommended option); logged in `docs/DESIGN.md`. Every name was checked against the show's material (`docs/DESIGN.md` §2) and the words Richard's other books use.

Rules are in `docs/CORE-RULES.md`. Where this file and the rules disagree, the rules win.

## The city: Larkhaven

An invented harbour city, today. Fog in the mornings, ferries, steep streets, old money in the Heights and new flats on the old docks.

| Area | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Old Town** | Narrow streets, the Lantern House, St Bride's market hall, the night market on Fridays |
| **The Harbour** | Ferries, the fish market, the long pier, the lighthouse |
| **Saltmarsh** | Reed beds, the drowned village at low tide, birdwatchers |
| **The Heights** | Big houses, the old radio tower, the observatory |
| **University Hill** | Students, cheap cafés, the library with the locked basement |
| **The Docks** | New flats, a burned-out theatre, the container yard |

## The coven's home: the Lantern House

A narrow, three-storey house in Old Town with a lamp over the door that has never gone out. The Book lives in the attic. The kitchen is where potions get brewed. The guardian is bound to the house.

- **Ground floor:** front room, kitchen (brewing), back door to a walled garden.
- **First floor:** three bedrooms, bathroom.
- **Attic:** the Book on a lectern, old trunks, a round window over the street.
- Threats can't come inside uninvited. (Story only: the rules never put a showdown here.)

## The ready-made coven

Six witches, one per Power (the d6 order), and the guardian. Any witch can be any gender; these are just the ready-made ones. Ratings follow the rules (3, 2, 2, 1, with the 3 where the Power is used most).

| Witch | Power | Heart | Wits | Nerve | Craft | Life | Tie to the coven | Look |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dana Okafor** (she) | Force | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | Nurse | Grew up next door to the Lantern House | Tall, short natural hair, scrubs under a denim jacket; a silver ring on her left thumb |
| **Marco Rossi** (he) | Flame | 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 | Mechanic | Cousin of the family that owns the house | Stocky, oil on his hands, rolled sleeves; a burn scar on his right forearm |
| **June Takeda** (she) | Sight | 2 | 3 | 1 | 2 | Student | Found the Book in a second-hand shop and followed it home | Small, round glasses, a yellow raincoat; always carries a notebook |
| **Quinn Abernathy** (they) | Voice | 3 | 2 | 1 | 2 | Teacher | Old friend of Dana's from school | Lanky, curly red hair, cardigan, a whistle on a cord |
| **Tess Lindqvist** (she) | Veil | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 | Bus driver | Inherited a room in the house from her gran | Broad-shouldered, blonde braid, driver's uniform; a pale streak in her braid |
| **Noor Haddad** (she) | Wild | 2 | 2 | 1 | 3 | Shop worker (a garden centre) | Answered an advert for a lodger and stayed | Headscarf in green, soil under her nails; a crow feather in her pocket |

**The guardian: Rook.** A protective spirit bound to the Lantern House by an old promise. In human shape, a very tall, very still person in a long grey coat, grey-eyed, who speaks little. In animal shape, a large black rook. Heart 3, Wits 2, Nerve 2. It calls the witches "the household". Nothing like the show's guardian: no healing light, no flying off in sparkles, no council above it. When it Carries someone, the air folds like a page and they are elsewhere.

## Threats

Every threat uses the stat column of the episode's difficulty label (Defence, Danger, Hit, Toughness from `CORE-RULES.md`). What differs is the story, the Weakness, the places and the signature move (the d6 result of 6, unless the innocent is there and free).

| Threat | What it is | It wants (its plan) | Weakness | Hidden place | Public place | Signature move (on a 6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Quiet Man** | A demon in a grey suit who steals voices | Seven stolen voices to sing a door open | His own name spoken aloud, and salt | The old radio tower in the Heights | The Friday night market | **Static:** attack two different characters; the first one he hurts can't cast on their next action |
| **The Ash Widow** | A fire spirit in a burned wedding dress | To burn the house where she died, with a bride inside | Water from the harbour, blessed by a coven | The burned-out theatre on the Docks | The harbour fireworks | **Cinders:** attack every character, but Hit is 1 |
| **The Mirror Thief** | A thin creature that wears stolen faces | A face to keep forever | Its true reflection: make it look in an old mirror | Behind the mirrors of a closed department store | St Bride's market hall | **Borrowed Face:** attack the witch with the most Magic left, with Hit 1 higher; on a Failure they also lose 1 Magic |
| **The Night Nurse** | A creature that feeds on the sleeping sick | One more night of feeding before the moon turns | Iron, and being called by the name of someone it killed | The closed wing of Larkhaven General | The hospital car park at shift change | **Drain:** one attack; if it does any Hurt, the threat clears 2 damage |
| **Vesper Crane** | A witch who stole other witches' power | The coven's Book | The spell she broke to steal her power, read back to her | The library's locked basement | The university graduation | **Turned Spell:** one attack with Hit 1 higher |
| **The Drowned Choir** | Ghosts of a village the sea took | One more voice for the choir | A bell from the drowned church, rung at low tide | The drowned village in Saltmarsh at low tide | The long pier at dusk | **Lure:** one attack; then the standing character with the lowest Heart rolls Heart against Danger (no Hurt on a Partial or a Failure); on a Failure they lose their next action |

## Ready-made episodes

| Episode | Label | Threat | Innocent | Opening |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Night Market** | Easy | The Quiet Man | Grace Ellery, a radio host whose voice is famous across Larkhaven | A singer at the night market opens her mouth and nothing comes out |
| **2. Cinders** | Standard | The Ash Widow | Hannah Price, a firefighter, getting married next week | A row of old houses burns in the shape of a wedding ring |
| **3. Low Tide** | Hard | The Drowned Choir | **Finn Marlow**, a twelve-year-old who sings in the school choir | Three people walk into the sea at dusk, singing |

Each episode in the book gives: the opening, the innocent, the threat, the Clues needed, six leads (two talking, two searching, one Book research, one for Reveal) with what each kind of Clue tells, a d6 table of Life calls for that episode, the hidden place and the public place (each with two ways in and out), one twist, and a closing scene.

## Episode generator (d6 tables)

Roll each table once. Any result works with any other.

| d6 | Threat | Innocent | Plan (what happens at Doom 12) | Public place | Hidden place | Twist |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Quiet Man | A local celebrity | Takes the innocent away for good | The night market | An abandoned building | A witch's Life is tied to the innocent |
| 2 | The Ash Widow | A child | Opens a door for something worse | A festival | Under the city (tunnels, basements) | A second, weaker threat is helping |
| 3 | The Mirror Thief | A witch's relative | Takes the innocent's face, voice or name | A hospital | A place the sea or river covers | The innocent doesn't want to be saved |
| 4 | The Night Nurse | A stranger with a gift | Feeds and grows stronger (next time it returns, +2 Toughness) | A school or university | A closed shop or theatre | The threat was once a witch |
| 5 | Vesper Crane | Someone who saw too much | Steals something from the coven (the Book, a memory) | A ferry, pier or station | The old radio tower or the observatory | A reporter is following the coven (start with Exposure 1) |
| 6 | The Drowned Choir | Someone the threat once loved | Ends a family line | A wedding, funeral or party | Somewhere the coven knows well | None |
