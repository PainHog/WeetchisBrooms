# Witch & Ward: glossary

One meaning per word (DESIGN-RULES A16). Every term here was checked against the show's material in `docs/DESIGN.md` §2 and against the words Richard's other books already use (the Hunt, Clash, Harm, Awakening, Bonded, Warden, Priya). The rules are in the rulebook chapters; `docs/CORE-RULES.md` is the one-page summary.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | Any attempt to damage the threat (a roll against its Defence), or the threat's go at a character. Not a stat. |
| **Casting** | Using a Power, a spell, a potion or a guardian gift. In a public showdown, any round with casting raises Exposure by 1; out in the city, a casting roll's cost is Exposure. Hide never raises Exposure. |
| **Book, the** | The coven's spellbook, kept at home. Research there gives +1 die; destroyed threats get a page. Not the show's book or its name. |
| **Broken** | A threat whose Toughness is full: it stops attacking, Doom stops, and it flees at the end of the next round unless the Unmaking destroys it. |
| **Carry** | The guardian's gift that takes one person to a place it knows; the guardian is back at once. Story only in the investigation. |
| **Clue** | One step of the investigation. Half the Clues tell the coven what the threat is and where; all of them reveal its Weakness. |
| **Cost** | What a Partial or a Failure brings: 1 Hurt in a showdown; out of one, Exposure +1 for casting out in the city, otherwise 1 Hurt. |
| **Coven** | The group of witches (2–5), plus the guardian if a player plays it. Family by blood or by choice. |
| **Craft** | The rating for spells and potions. |
| **Critical** | A Success that shows two or more 6s. |
| **Danger** (threat stat) | The Difficulty of defending against the threat's attacks. |
| **Defence** (threat stat) | The Difficulty of hurting the threat. |
| **Difficulty** | The number of successes a roll needs (usually 1–4; the Unmaking can be higher). |
| **Doom** | The threat's plan, 12 boxes. Rises after every scene and round. Has the Showdown mark at 5. |
| **Down** | A character whose Hurt boxes are full: out of action until healed below full. Never dead. |
| **Driven off / fled** | A Broken threat that got away before the Unmaking worked. It can come back. |
| **Episode** | One session's story, from opening to closing. |
| **Exposure** | How close ordinary people are to seeing magic. When full, the coven is **Seen**. |
| **Failure** | Fewer successes than half the Difficulty. It doesn't work and there's a cost. |
| **Free** | A showdown action: Nerve against Defence to pull the innocent out of the threat's grip; only a Success works. |
| **Gift** | One of the guardian's abilities: Mend, Shield, Carry, Sense, Hold. |
| **Group check** | A roll everyone makes when everyone must do the same thing. Not an action. The group's result comes from how many got a Success or a Partial; one cost at most. |
| **Guard** | A showdown action: until the end of the threat's next turn, if it goes for the freed innocent, it attacks you instead. It ends if you go Down. |
| **Guardian** | The coven's protective spirit, bound to its home by an old promise; human or animal shape; no masters. Never called a "Warden". |
| **Hidden place** | Where a showdown happens when the coven goes to the threat. The innocent isn't there, and casting doesn't raise Exposure. |
| **Heart** | The rating for people: talking, comforting, lying, reading people. |
| **Hit** (threat stat) | The Hurt a threat deals when a defence fails. |
| **Hurt** | Damage to a character: 4 boxes for a witch, 5 for the guardian. Used instead of "Harm", which another of Richard's books uses. |
| **In its grip** | The innocent at the start of a public showdown. Lost if still in the threat's grip at the end of the first round. |
| **Innocent** | The person the threat wants this episode. |
| **Label** | An episode's difficulty: Easy, Standard or Hard. It sets the threat's numbers from the table. Not the same as a roll's Difficulty. |
| **Lead** | An investigation action that can find Clues: a talking lead, a searching lead (researching the Book is one) or Reveal. |
| **Lead Difficulty** | The Difficulty of leads and brewing, set by the label. |
| **Life** | A witch's everyday job or role. It calls once per episode. |
| **Loss** | An episode's result: Doom filled, every witch was Down, or the coven retreated. |
| **Magic** | A witch's points, 5 per episode. (Using magic in general is **casting**.) |
| **Near** | In the same place. In a showdown, everyone is near. Moving takes no action. |
| **Nerve** | The rating for moving, fighting and standing firm. |
| **Partial** | A roll's result: at least half the Difficulty but not all of it; it works, with a cost. Also an episode's result: the threat fled, or it was destroyed but the innocent was lost. |
| **Power** | Each witch's one gift: Force, Flame, Sight, Voice, Veil or Wild. |
| **Public place** | Where a showdown happens when the threat comes to the coven. The innocent is there, in its grip, and every round with casting raises Exposure. |
| **Retreat** | Leaving a showdown together at the start of a round. A Loss, but nobody gets a Scar. |
| **Round** | One step of the showdown: everyone acts, the threat acts, Doom rises. In round 1 the threat acts first. |
| **Scar** | 1 Hurt carried into the next episode by a witch who was Down when the coven lost. |
| **Scene** | One step of the investigation: everyone acts, Doom rises. The whole showdown counts as one scene for once-per-scene limits. |
| **Searching lead** | A lead using Wits: going over a scene, reading records, researching the Book. |
| **Season** | A run of episodes with carry-over. |
| **Seen** | Exposure is full; the episode's result drops one step, and any further rise in Exposure becomes Doom +1 (once per round or scene). |
| **Showdown** | The fight with the threat. |
| **Showdown mark** | Doom 5: if Doom reaches it, the threat comes to the coven in a public place. |
| **Signature move** | What a threat does on a 6. Each threat has its own (Chapter 10). |
| **Spell** | Reveal, Bind, Blast, Shelter, Hide or the Unmaking (Craft, 1 Magic). |
| **Storyteller** | The person running the game. |
| **Success** | A roll's result: at least as many successes as the Difficulty. With a capital S. In lower case, a success is one die showing 4, 5 or 6. |
| **Surge** | A Power's bigger effect, 2 Magic, once per scene. |
| **Talking lead** | A lead using Heart: questioning, comforting, lying, reading people. |
| **Threat** | The episode's enemy: a demon, a spirit, a rogue witch, a cursed thing or a creature. |
| **Toughness** | The damage that Breaks a threat. |
| **Turn** | A character's turn is when they take their action in a scene or a round; characters act in any order. The threat's turn is when it makes its attacks in a round. |
| **Unmaking** | The joined spell that destroys a Broken threat. |
| **Ward** | The guardian's points, 5 per episode. (Only this meaning in the rules; the title's "Ward" is protection in general.) |
| **Weakness** | What the coven learns with all the Clues. Without it the threat's Defence is 1 higher and the Unmaking is 2 harder; with it, attack rolls that work do 1 more damage. |
| **Win** | An episode's result: the threat was destroyed, and the innocent is safe. |
| **Witch** | A player character with a Power. |
| **Wits** | The rating for noticing, searching and research. |
