Chapter 3

Witches

Every witch in the coven has one Power, a life outside magic and people who count on them. Some were born to it. Some woke up one morning and set the curtains on fire. Making a witch takes five steps and about ten minutes.

The four ratings

A witch has four ratings. Each one is the number of dice you roll for that kind of action.

RatingWhat it coversRolls that use it
HeartPeople: talking, comforting, lying, reading peopleTalking leads
WitsNoticing, searching, researchSearching leads, researching the Book, studying the threat
NerveMoving, fighting, standing firmAttacks, freeing the innocent, defending
CraftSpells and potionsEvery spell, including the Unmaking; brewing

The guardian has only Heart, Wits and Nerve (Chapter 4).

Making a witch

1. Your Power

Roll a d6 on this table, or pick. No two witches share a Power. If you roll one that another witch already has, roll again.

d6PowerIn short
1ForceMove things with your mind
2FlameCall fire
3SightGlimpses of what’s coming or what happened
4VoiceWords that sway minds
5VeilGo unseen, hide magic
6WildTalk to animals, grow and bind with plants

2. Your ratings

Put 3, 2, 2 and 1 into Heart, Wits, Nerve and Craft, in any order. A good habit is to put the 3 where your Power is used most: Nerve for Force or Flame, Wits for Sight, Heart for Voice, Craft for Wild. Veil works with Nerve, Wits and Craft, so any of those suits it.

3. Your Life

Your Life is your job or role outside magic. Roll a d6, or pick. If you roll one another witch already has, roll again.

d6Life
1Student
2Nurse
3Shop worker
4Teacher
5Mechanic
6Bus driver

4. Your tie to the coven

Decide how your witch is family to the others, by blood or by choice. A sister, a cousin, an old friend from school, the neighbour’s kid who never went home, someone who inherited a room in the house, a lodger who stayed. Ties have no rules. They tell everyone why you would run into a burning theatre for each other.

5. Your name and look

Choose a name and pronouns. Any witch can be any gender. Then pick one or two things people notice: a yellow raincoat, oil on your hands, a silver ring on your thumb.

Then fill in your sheet

Every witch has 4 Hurt boxes, all empty, and starts each episode with 5 Magic.

The six Powers

Each Power adds 2 dice to a roll it fits (Chapter 2). Using your Power is casting. Each Power also has a Surge: a bigger effect.

Surges

Once per scene, you may Surge. It costs 2 Magic, paid before you roll, and it’s spent even if you get a Failure. Add it to an action that uses your Power. It works on a Partial or better. The whole showdown counts as one scene, so you get one Surge per showdown.

A talking lead uses Heart. A searching lead uses Wits.

Force

You move things with your mind: throw, push, pull, slam a door from across the room.

Fits: attacks, freeing the innocent. Surge: the threat loses its next attack.

Flame

You call fire: a spark, a lash of flame, a candle that won’t go out.

Fits: attacks. Surge: 2 more damage.

Sight

You catch glimpses of what’s coming or what happened: a flash of the past when you touch a doorframe, a warning a second before the blow lands.

Fits: leads, defending, studying the threat. Surge: 1 more Clue. Or, with no action, before the defence roll, one attack aimed at anyone misses. That miss isn’t a lost attack.

Voice

Your words sway minds: a witness who opens up, a crowd that decides it saw nothing.

Fits: talking leads, Hide. Surge: 1 more Clue, or Exposure −1.

Veil

You go unseen, and you hide magic: people look straight past you, and a spell becomes a trick of the light.

Fits: defending, searching leads, Hide. Surge: no Exposure from casting for the rest of the scene, or in a showdown for this round and the next. While it lasts, a casting roll’s cost becomes 1 Hurt.

Wild

You talk to animals, and you grow and bind with plants: the gulls tell you what they saw, ivy wraps a demon’s legs.

Fits: talking and searching leads, Bind, brewing. Surge: 1 more Clue or potion. Or, in a showdown, the threat’s Defence is 1 lower for everyone this round.

Magic

You start every episode with 5 Magic. There is no top limit: answering your Life gives you 1 more (below). Spend Magic like this:

SpendMagic
Cast a spell1
Surge your Power2
Help someone else’s roll (+1 die)1
Add a die to your own roll1
Lead or join the Unmaking1

Unspent Magic is lost at the end of the episode, so spend it. But keep 2 for the showdown: 1 to join the Unmaking, and 1 for a die or a second try (Chapter 7).

Hurt and Down

You mark Hurt when a roll’s cost says so and when the threat’s attacks land. A witch has 4 Hurt boxes. Hurt can’t go past full.

Down

When all your Hurt boxes are full, you are Down. You can take no actions, except resting during the investigation. You can still help other people’s rolls and join the Unmaking. You are back up as soon as your Hurt drops below full.

Three things clear Hurt: resting in a scene clears all of it, a Healing potion clears 2 (Chapter 5) and the guardian’s Mend clears 2 (Chapter 4).

Nobody dies from the dice. But Down has a real cost. If every witch is Down at the end of a showdown round, the coven loses (Chapter 7). And a witch who is Down when the coven loses gets a Scar: 1 Hurt at the start of the next episode (Chapter 8).

Your Life

Your Life pulls at you. It helps in two ways and costs in one.

  • It adds a die. When your Life fits a roll and the Storyteller agrees, add 1 die (inside the +2 limit).
  • It calls. Once per episode, your Life calls: a double shift, a parents’ evening, a bus that won’t start, a friend in trouble.

The Life call

You can answer your Life in any scene of the investigation. Answering takes your action for that scene, needs no roll, and gives you 1 Magic. If you haven’t answered by the showdown, you lose 1 Magic, or take 1 Hurt if you have no Magic.

Answering costs a lead. Ignoring it costs Magic you’ll want in the fight. Either way, play the scene: a missed date or a covered shift is good story.

Example: making a witch

Dana (Force) and June (Sight) are already in the coven. Kemi is making the third witch.

Power. She rolls a 1: Force, which Dana has. She rolls again: 6, Wild.

Ratings. Wild fits talking and searching leads, Bind and brewing. Kemi puts 3 in Craft for Bind and brewing, 2 in Heart, 2 in Wits and 1 in Nerve. With Nerve 1 her witch will defend badly in a fight, so she plans to keep a Healing potion close.

Life. She rolls a 2: nurse, which Dana has. She rolls again: 4, teacher.

Tie. Her witch taught June at school and now rents the box room at the Lantern House.

Name and look. Nell Ashby (she), freckled, with a bike helmet clipped to her bag and a sprig of rosemary behind one ear.

Kemi marks 4 empty Hurt boxes and 5 Magic. Nell is ready.
