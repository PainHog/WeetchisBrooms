Magic is real. Keep it quiet.

In the harbour city of Larkhaven, a lamp burns over the door of an old house in Old Town. The coven lives there. By day they are nurses, students, mechanics and bus drivers. By night they hunt the threat of the week: demons, spirits and stranger things, each one after someone innocent.

Follow leads, read the Book and brew potions. Face the threat with spells and your own Power, then finish it together. One player can be the guardian, a spirit bound to the house. And keep magic secret, because the city is watching.

3–5 players and a Storyteller · ages 13+ · 2–3 hours
