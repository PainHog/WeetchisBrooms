Notes

Quick Reference

The roll

Roll dice equal to the rating. Each 4, 5 or 6 is a success. The Storyteller sets the Difficulty (usually 1–4; the Unmaking can be higher).

Success: successes equal to the Difficulty or more. Critical: a success that shows two or more 6s. It does one thing extra: +1 damage, +1 Clue, +1 potion or −1 more Exposure, as fits; otherwise +1 die on your next roll this episode (inside the +2 limit).

Partial: at least half the Difficulty (round up), but less than all of it. It works, with a cost. (At Difficulty 1 there is no Partial.) Failure: fewer than that. It doesn’t work, and there is a cost. Group check: when everyone must do the same thing, everyone rolls; it isn’t an action. One cost, taken by whoever rolled worst.

Costs

In a showdown, 1 Hurt. Otherwise, Exposure +1 if you were casting out in the city, or else 1 Hurt. Casting means using a Power, a spell, a potion or a guardian gift. A helper’s die or Sense doesn’t make your roll casting. Casting Hide never raises Exposure, so its cost is always 1 Hurt. Spells (Craft) cost 1 Magic; a Surge costs 2 Magic.

Extra dice

Your Power adds 2 dice when it fits (you may leave it off; then the roll isn’t casting). On top of that, at most +2 dice from everything else together: helpers (1 Magic or Ward each; helping takes no action, so anyone can help, even after acting or while Down), your Life (+1 when the Storyteller agrees it fits), the Book (+1), or your own Magic (Ward for the guardian; 1 each). Sense’s die doesn’t count toward the limit.

Investigation (scenes)

Each character takes one action: follow a lead out in the city (Heart, Wits or Reveal against the Lead Difficulty: a success gives 1 Clue, a Critical 2, a Partial 1 with a cost); research the Book at home (a lead using Wits, +1 die, with no Exposure; one character per scene, who may also make one brewing roll that scene; each roll has its own result and cost); brew a potion at home; rest (clear all Hurt); cover up out in the city (Hide); or answer their Life (no roll; gain 1 Magic). Each witch’s Life calls once per episode, and they can answer it in any scene; if they haven’t by the showdown, they lose 1 Magic (or take 1 Hurt if they have none).

At the end of each scene, Doom +1. If Doom has reached the Showdown mark (5), the threat comes to the coven: a public place, with the innocent in its grip. If not, with half the Clues (rounded up) the coven knows what the threat is and where, and may go to it (this takes no scene): a hidden place, without the innocent. With all the Clues it knows the Weakness.

Showdown round order

Round 1: the threat’s attacks, the characters’ actions, the innocent, Exposure, Doom. Later rounds: the characters’ actions, the threat’s attacks, Exposure, Doom. In a public place, every round in which anyone was casting raises Exposure by 1 (once per round). The coven may retreat at the start of any round (everyone leaves together).

Actions (one each, any order, or nothing)

Attack: Nerve, or Craft with Blast; a Power that fits adds 2 dice; against Defence: a success does 2 damage, a Critical 3, a Partial 1 with a cost; a Failure costs 1 Hurt.

Free the innocent: Nerve against Defence; only a success frees them (a Partial doesn’t, and still costs 1 Hurt). If they’re still in its grip at the end of the first round, they’re lost.

Guard the innocent: until the end of the threat’s next turn, if it goes for them it attacks you instead; it ends if you go Down. Study the threat: Wits against Defence: 1 Clue. Or cast a spell, or use a gift. Weakness: until the coven knows it, the threat’s Defence is 1 higher. Once it knows it, attack rolls (including Blast) that work do 1 more damage; potions don’t.

The threat acts

It makes 1 attack a round against 3 characters, 2 against 4 or 5. It loses at most one attack a round. Against a coven of 3 witches and no guardian player, it has 3 more Toughness. For each attack, roll a d6:

1–3: it attacks whoever hurt it last (if nobody has, or they’re Down, use 4–5).
4–5: whoever has the most Hurt marked and is still standing (ties: the Storyteller picks).
6: its signature move (if it has none, it attacks two different characters), but if the innocent is there and free it goes for them instead: it attacks their guard, or takes them if nobody is guarding them (taking them isn’t an attack, so Hold can’t stop it).

The target rolls Nerve (plus a Power that fits) against Danger: Success no Hurt, Partial 1 Hurt, Failure Hit Hurt. When its Toughness is full, it is Broken: it makes no more rolls or moves, Doom stops rising, and it flees at the end of the next round.

The Unmaking (only against a Broken threat)

One standing witch leads with Craft as their action and pays 1 Magic. Every other witch may join for 1 Magic, and each one adds 2 dice; joiners don’t count toward the +2 limit. A standing witch joins as their action, so must not have acted this round; a Down witch joins without one. Difficulty: the number of witches in the coven, +2 without the Weakness. Only a success destroys the threat. On a Partial or Failure it isn’t destroyed and nobody takes Hurt: try again next round if it’s still there.

The guardian’s gifts (1 Ward each)

Mend (action): clear 2 Hurt from someone it touches. Shield (no action, once a round): take an attack meant for someone near it (in a showdown, everyone is near). Carry (action): take one person to a place it knows; it can’t carry the coven out of a showdown or past a threat. Sense (no action, once a scene): +1 die on someone else’s lead this scene, outside the +2 limit. Hold (action, no Ward, not casting): Nerve against Defence; if it works, the threat’s next attack must target the guardian. No guardian player: once per episode, on any character’s turn, the coven may call it for one Mend or one Carry; calling takes no action and no roll, and counts as casting.

The end

The episode ends at once when the threat is destroyed, flees, or Doom fills, when every witch is Down at the end of a showdown round, or when the coven retreats. Win: destroyed, and the innocent safe. Partial: it fled, or it was destroyed but the innocent was lost. Loss: Doom filled, every witch Down, or retreat. Seen drops the result one step (a Loss stays a Loss). On a retreat, or when every witch is Down, an innocent still in the fight is lost. A witch who is Down when the coven loses (except by retreating) gets a Scar: 1 Hurt at the start of the next episode.
