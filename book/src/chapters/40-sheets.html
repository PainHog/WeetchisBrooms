Quick Reference

The core rules at a glance. The chapters have the full text.

The roll

Roll dice equal to the rating. Each 4, 5 or 6 is a success. The Storyteller sets the Difficulty (usually 1–4; the Unmaking can be higher).

  • Success: successes ≥ Difficulty. Critical: a success that shows two or more 6s. It does one thing extra: +1 damage, +1 Clue, +1 potion or −1 more Exposure, as fits; otherwise +1 die on your next roll this episode (inside the +2 limit).
  • Partial: at least half the Difficulty (round up), but less than all of it. It works, with a cost. (At Difficulty 1 there is no Partial.)
  • Failure: fewer than that. It doesn’t work, and there is a cost.
  • Group check: when everyone must do the same thing, everyone rolls; it isn’t an action. One cost, taken by whoever rolled worst.

Costs

In a showdown, 1 Hurt. Otherwise, Exposure +1 if you were casting out in the city, or else 1 Hurt. Casting means using a Power, a spell, a potion or a guardian gift. A helper’s die or Sense doesn’t make your roll casting. Casting Hide never raises Exposure, so its cost is always 1 Hurt.

Spending: a spell 1 Magic · a Surge 2 Magic · help 1 Magic or Ward · a die on your own roll 1 Magic (Ward for the guardian) · lead or join the Unmaking 1 Magic · a gift 1 Ward (Hold: none).

Extra dice

Your Power adds 2 dice when it fits (you may leave it off; then the roll isn’t casting). On top of that, at most +2 dice from everything else together: helpers (1 Magic or Ward each; helping takes no action, so anyone can help, even after acting or while Down), your Life (+1 when the Storyteller agrees it fits), the Book (+1), or your own Magic (Ward for the guardian; 1 each). Sense’s die doesn’t count toward the limit.

Investigation (scenes)

Each character takes one action:

  • follow a lead out in the city (Heart, Wits or Reveal against the Lead Difficulty: a success gives 1 Clue, a Critical 2, a Partial 1 with a cost);
  • research the Book at home (a lead using Wits, +1 die, with no Exposure; one character per scene, who may also make one brewing roll that scene);
  • brew a potion at home; rest (clear all Hurt); cover up out in the city (Hide); or answer their Life (no roll; gain 1 Magic).

A Life calls once per episode; if it isn’t answered by the showdown, lose 1 Magic (or take 1 Hurt if you have none). At the end of each scene, Doom +1. If Doom has reached the Showdown mark (5), the threat comes to the coven: a public place, the innocent in its grip. If not, with half the Clues (rounded up) the coven knows what and where, and may go to it (no scene): a hidden place, without the innocent. All the Clues: the Weakness.

Showdown round order

Round 1: the threat’s attacks, the characters’ actions, the innocent, Exposure, Doom. Later rounds: the characters’ actions, the threat’s attacks, Exposure, Doom. In a public place, every round in which anyone was casting raises Exposure by 1 (once per round). The coven may retreat at the start of any round (everyone leaves together).

Actions (one each, any order, or nothing)

  • Attack: Nerve, or Craft with Blast; a Power that fits adds 2 dice; against Defence: a success does 2 damage, a Critical 3, a Partial 1 with a cost; a Failure costs 1 Hurt.
  • Cast a spell or use a gift.
  • Free the innocent: Nerve against Defence; only a success frees them (a Partial doesn’t, and still costs 1 Hurt). Still in its grip at the end of the first round: lost.
  • Guard the innocent: until the end of the threat’s next turn, if it goes for them it attacks you instead; it ends if you go Down.
  • Study the threat: Wits against Defence: 1 Clue.

Weakness: until it’s known, Defence is 1 higher; once known, attack rolls (including Blast) that work do 1 more damage; potions don’t.

The threat acts

1 attack a round against 3 characters, 2 against 4 or 5. It loses at most one attack a round. For each attack, roll a d6:

  • 1–3: it attacks whoever hurt it last (if nobody has, or they’re Down, use 4–5).
  • 4–5: whoever has the most Hurt marked and is still standing (ties: the Storyteller picks).
  • 6: its signature move (if it has none, it attacks two different characters), but if the innocent is there and free it goes for them instead: it attacks their guard, or takes them if nobody is guarding them (taking them isn’t an attack, so Hold can’t stop it).

The target rolls Nerve (plus a Power that fits) against Danger: Success no Hurt, Partial 1 Hurt, Failure Hit Hurt. Toughness full: Broken. It makes no more rolls or moves, Doom stops rising, and it flees at the end of the next round.

The Unmaking (only against a Broken threat)

One standing witch leads with Craft as their action and pays 1 Magic. Every other witch may join for 1 Magic, and each one adds 2 dice; joiners don’t count toward the +2 limit. A standing witch joins as their action; a Down witch joins without one. Difficulty: the number of witches in the coven, +2 without the Weakness. Only a success destroys the threat. On a Partial or Failure nobody takes Hurt: try again next round if it’s still there.

The guardian’s gifts (1 Ward each)

Mend (action): clear 2 Hurt from someone it touches. Shield (no action, once a round): take an attack meant for someone near it. Carry (action): take one person to a place it knows; not the coven out of a showdown or past a threat. Sense (no action): +1 die on a lead this scene, outside the +2 limit. Hold (action, no Ward): Nerve against Defence; if it works, the threat’s next attack must target the guardian. No guardian player: once per episode, the coven may call it for one Mend or one Carry (no action, no roll; counts as casting).

The end

The episode ends at once when the threat is destroyed, flees, or Doom fills, when every witch is Down at the end of a showdown round, or when the coven retreats.

  • Win: destroyed, and the innocent safe. Partial: it fled, or it was destroyed but the innocent was lost. Loss: Doom filled, every witch Down, or retreat.
  • Seen drops the result one step (a Loss stays a Loss).
  • A witch who is Down when the coven loses gets a Scar: 1 Hurt at the start of the next episode.
