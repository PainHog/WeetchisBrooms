# Witch & Ward — rulings and text changes

Every ruling and every change to the rules text gets a row here: what changed, where,
why, and the source (author / playtest / simulator).

| 1 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The Vanquish | Each joining witch adds 2 dice (was 1). Difficulty is the number of witches in the coven, +2 without the Weakness (was a fixed number per label).
