# Witch & Ward — rulings and text changes

Every ruling and every change to the rules text gets a row here: what changed, where,
why, and the source (author / playtest / simulator).

| # | Date | Where | What changed | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The Vanquish | Each joining witch adds 2 dice (was 1). Difficulty is the number of witches in the coven, +2 without the Weakness (was a fixed number per label). Only a success destroys the threat; a Partial or Failure has no other cost (was: a Partial destroys it and costs each joiner 1 Hurt) | With 1 die per joiner against a fixed Difficulty, 5 witches almost never failed and 2 often did; group sizes differed by about 20 points. Now every size is within a few points | simulator |
| 2 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: [T] Toughness | Toughness counts witches only (was 2–3 more for a guardian player) | A guardian player did 10–20 points worse than one more witch | simulator |
| 3 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: [T] Clues needed | 3 Clues per character plus 1 / 2 / 4 by label (was n + 2) | The coven knew the Weakness after 2 scenes, the Showdown mark was never reached and Doom never pressed (A22) | simulator |
| 4 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: [T] table | Retuned: Lead Difficulty 1/2/2; Defence and Attack 1/2/2; Hit 2/2/3; Toughness 3w/3w/4w; Exposure 5/4/4; Doom 12 with the mark at 5 for every label (was 12/10/9 with marks 6/5/4); Vanquish row removed (see 1) | To meet the win and loss targets (sim/REPORT.md §1) with a simpler table | simulator |
| 5 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: Spells | Reveal gives 1 more Clue on a success; Blast does 1 more damage if it works | Reveal was never the best lead and Blast was almost never the best attack: dead options (A9) | simulator |
| 6 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: Powers | Voice's Surge can give 1 more Clue on a talking lead, as well as Exposure −1 | Voice's Surge was almost never worth using (A9) | simulator |
| 7 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: Powers | A Surge is added to an action using the Power and works on a Partial or better; the whole showdown counts as one scene. Sight's fight Surge needs no action | v0.1 didn't say when a Surge works or what "once per scene" means in a showdown (A10) | simulator |
| 8 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The roll, Cost | In a showdown the cost is always 1 Hurt; out of a showdown, Exposure +1 if magic was used out in the city, otherwise 1 Hurt | v0.1 could charge Exposure twice in a public showdown (A2) | simulator |
| 9 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The roll | "Magic" means a Power, a spell, a potion or a guardian gift | The public Exposure rule needs to know what counts | simulator |
| 10 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: Investigation | Leads are out in the city; Book research (one character per scene) and brewing are at home; Hide is out in the city; resting is the one action a Down character can take | v0.1 didn't say where leads happen, how many can use the Book, or what a Down character can do | simulator |
| 11 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The threat | On a 6 with the innocent there, the threat attacks their guard or takes them; taking them isn't an attack, so Hold can't stop it | Order of resolution (A15) | simulator |
| 12 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The guardian | It can't join the Vanquish (as DESIGN Q2 says); a Down guardian fades back to the home for the rest of the scene | Stated outright (A10, A16) | simulator |
| 13 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: Characters | Advice: spend Magic, but keep 2 for the Vanquish | Covens that spent down to 0 couldn't pay for the Vanquish and the threat fled (A7, A18) | simulator |
| 14 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The innocent | In a public showdown the threat starts holding the innocent. Freeing them is Nerve against Defence and needs a full success (Force fits); if they're still held at the end of round 1 they're lost. A held innocent can't be guarded or carried; a Broken threat lets go | The innocent was almost never lost, so keeping them safe wasn't a real choice | Richard (approved recommendation), tuned by simulator |
| 15 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: The Weakness | Until the coven knows the Weakness, the threat's Defence is 1 higher; once it knows, attacks that work do 1 more damage (the Vanquish's +2 without it stays) | Going in early without the Weakness beat waiting for it; now the two are within about a point, and rushing in with half the Clues loses badly | Richard (approved recommendation), tuned by simulator |
| 16 | 2026-10-30 | CORE-RULES: [T] Clues needed | 3 × n at Easy, 4 × n − 1 at Standard, 4 × n at Hard (was 3n + 1 / 3n + 2 / 3n + 4) | The fixed add-on made small covens miss the Weakness far more often; with the Weakness now mattering more, 3-player covens fell 20 points behind at Hard | simulator |
