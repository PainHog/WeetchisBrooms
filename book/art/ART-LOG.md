# Art log: part pages, chapter vignettes and spots (TEMP test art)

The `ART-CHECKLIST.md` audit for the 32 pieces made by `book/tools/art-gen/scenes/vignettes.mjs`
(3 part pages, 16 chapter vignettes, 13 spots). Each piece was looked at full size (2× and 4×
renders, with close crops of every figure's hands and face) and at print size (a vignette at
7.1in × 1.8in, a part piece at 6.7in wide, each spot at its placed height of 3.0–5.3in in the
built PDF). The cover, back cover, portraits and maps are another artist's; they are not logged here.

## How to read the tables

- **Object table:** every object, its contact points, and what is under or holding it. "OK" means
  every end, edge and foot is supported, inside its support's outline, or deliberately cut by the frame.
- **Character rows:** E = eyes, Ea = ears, A = arms, H = hands, L = legs/feet. "5f" = five fingers
  (four fingers and a thumb, thumb toward the body's centre line, or on top of a level forearm).
  "mitt" = a small figure (under about 1in at print size) drawn with a simple hand and thumb, by design.
  "L/R" compares the figure with the reference sheet below.
- Every figure that stands has a contact shadow under its feet. Every hanging thing meets its hook
  or bracket; every rope or cord is anchored at both ends or leaves the frame.

**Profiles (for future pieces):** in a left-facing profile, Marco's scar, Tess's braid and streak and
Dana's earring on her right side are on the side facing the viewer; Dana's ring, Marco's rag, Tess's
badge and Noor's feather are on the far side (hidden or just showing). Check this before drawing a profile.

**Threats (countable features):** the Quiet Man (2 eyes, pale; 2 arms, 5f; grey suit and hat; a glass
radio valve held in his LEFT hand). The Ash Widow (2 eyes, ember orange; 2 arms, 5f where large; a
dress to the ground, hem burned and glowing; veil; bun with a half-burned blossom band). The Mirror
Thief (a faceted mirror head, no eyes, ears or hair; 2 long arms, 5 long fingers each; 2 legs).
The Night Nurse (2 dark-ringed eyes; 2 arms, 5f; veil cap, navy cape, white uniform; a white lily in
her LEFT hand). Vesper Crane (2 eyes; 2 arms, 5f; silver bob; long plum coat; necklace of five vials).
The Drowned Choir (each ghost: 2 eyes, mouth open singing; 2 arms at the sides fading out below the
waist into water, mud or fog, with the rest of the ghost; no legs shown).

**Show check:** no knot symbols anywhere; the Book's cover is plain (brass corners, a violet ribbon);
the rulebook's emblem is a small lantern; no glowing blue lights or sparkles near Rook; the Lantern
House is a narrow brick terrace, not a large detached house with a tower. No real faith symbols.
