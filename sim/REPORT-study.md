# Witch & Ward: balance study of the remaining misses

Rules: the book as it is (v0.9, `docs/CORE-RULES.md` and the chapters, rulings to 105). Command: `node sim/study.mjs --runs 2000 --seed 1`. 2,000 episodes per cell, the same seed and runs as `REPORT.md`, so every "book as it is" row is REPORT.md's numbers. Same seed, same report.

**This is a study. It changes no rule, number or default.** Every option below is a setting the simulator already has, run on a copy of the defaults. A few options are player habits the book already allows, or rules the simulator has no setting for. Those run on a copy of the engine with small switches, each off by default. With every switch off, the copy gives exactly the same results as `grid.mjs`: checked, identical. Options marked ‡ change the numbers table in Chapter 6 and need Richard's approval. All other options change a chapter's wording, or only how the simulated players play.

How to read the option tables: each row is one full run over the grid (18 cells, 36,000 episodes), with every REPORT.md §1 target. Numbers in **bold** miss their target. Win, loss and the other shares are % of episodes. Cells are the lowest and highest cell at each label. Guardian − witch is the worst of 3 / 4 / 5 players, in points of win. Ward and Magic are the median share spent. Potions used is REPORT.md's measure (potions made, including any lost to the carry limit). Public fights are pooled over all labels. Powers is the worst Power's distance from the average.

## 2. Easy win ~90% (now 84.8%)

**Why.** Easy has no losses (0.0%). Every Easy episode that isn't a Win is a threat that fled, because the one Unmaking try failed. The coven waits for the round after the threat Breaks, so that every witch can join; then the threat flees at the end of that round. The Unmaking hit rate by cell is 86.1 / 89.9 / 82.8 / 86.0 / 80.3 / 83.5% (3 players, 3 + guardian, 4, 4 + guardian, 5, 5 + guardian), and the Easy win by cell follows it exactly: 86.1 / 89.9 / 82.7 / 86.0 / 80.2 / 83.5. So Toughness, Clues and the Lead Difficulty barely move the Easy win (Toughness 4 × w: 85.1%). Only the Unmaking does.

A second try doesn't help on its own: with a Broken threat staying a round longer, the coven has spent the Magic it needs on the first try (85.6%).

The pooled miss is small (84.8% against 85%) and within seed noise (seeds 2 and 3: 85.3%, 85.9%). The cells for 4 and 5 witches (82.7%, 80.2%, and 83.5% for 4 witches + guardian) are the real miss.

## 10. Other findings

- **Chapter 7's Unmaking "Timing" advice costs wins.** It says: "If most of the coven has already acted, try now with whoever is left: a failed try costs no Hurt, only Magic." Played that way, the coven wins −8.7 / −13.3 / −11.8 points less (Easy / Standard / Hard). The small early try spends the Magic the full try needs next round. Suggested wording for Richard: "Unless it will flee first, wait for the next round, when every standing witch can join." This is advice, not a rule, but it changes how tables play.
- **The guardian row is near its limit on every seed.** Guardian − witch at Standard is +4.2 / +3.5 / +4.8 on seed 1, +2.8 / +6.3 / +4.1 on seed 2 and +5.0 / +7.8 / +6.0 on seed 3. One cell's win has about ±1 point of noise at 2,000 runs, so a difference of two cells has about ±1.4. A guardian player is worth about 4 points more than a witch at Easy and Standard, and that edge comes mostly from the Unmaking: a guardian coven has one witch fewer, so a lower Difficulty. REPORT.md's "yes" on that row is luck as much as balance. Consider more runs for that row, or a 6-point limit.
- **What the simulator should change, whatever Richard decides** (`sim/engine.mjs` policies, not rules): the guardian helps the Unmaking with Ward; brewers choose potion kinds. Both are play the book already allows, and each moves its own §1 row by 15–40 points. Until then, those two rows measure the simulated players more than the rules.
- **Each threat's signature move:** with the recommended Hard Toughness, Low Tide stays inside its band (§4). The other ready-made episodes use Easy and Standard, where the threat's move changes the win by less than a point (REPORT-threats.md), so this study runs them with the generic move.
