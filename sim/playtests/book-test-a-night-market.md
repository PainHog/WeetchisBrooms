# Book test A: first night, Episode 1: Night Market (rulebook v0.9)

- **Rules used:** the rulebook source only, `book/src/chapters/*.html` (v0.9 test edition), read in book order, plus the rulings logged in `book/REVIEW.md`. Nothing else was treated as a rule. Where the book gave no answer, the table made the ruling it would most likely make, and this log says so.
- **Table:** a Storyteller who has read the book once, and 3 players new to the game. Ready-made witches from Chapter 13. **No guardian player**, so the coven could call Rook once.
- **Episode:** Episode 1: Night Market (Chapter 14), at Easy, from the opening to the closing scene, run by Chapter 9's steps and Chapter 12's first-night advice.
- **Dice:** every roll came from a script (`Math.random`, one d6 per die), logged with a label at the moment it was rolled. There were 52 rolls: 16 in the episode (including the practice roll) and 36 in the drills. All 52 are in section 7, in order. No roll was made twice or thrown away.
- **Result:** **Win.** The Quiet Man was destroyed in round 2 of a hidden showdown at the old radio tower. Grace Ellery was never in danger. Exposure ended at 0 of 5, so the coven wasn't Seen. Nobody marked a single Hurt all night.

## 2. Play log

### Opening

The Storyteller read the opening aloud. Bea Santos goes silent on the steps of St Bride's, and the man in the grey suit walks away into the fog. Where were the witches standing? Dana was in the chip-shop queue after a day shift. June was at the record stall with her notebook. Noor was helping a friend at a plant stall, with a crow on the awning.

The players wanted to chase him (Sam), help Bea (Sam again) and send the crow after him (Alex). The book doesn't say what happens if the witches act in the opening (Issue C5). **Ruling:** the opening isn't a scene, so there are no actions and no rolls. The fog closes and he is gone, and what they wanted to do becomes the first leads of scene 1. Then the Life calls.

Tracks: Doom 0, Exposure 0 of 5, Clues 0 of 9. Dana 0 Hurt / 5 Magic, June 0 / 5, Noor 0 / 5. Potions: none.

## 5. What worked

- **Chapter 12's step list and checklist.** With pre-filled sheets, a Storyteller who had read the book once got three new players to the first scene in 32 minutes, with no character building. The tie-break for picking the same witch, the "guardian called" box and the practice roll were all there when needed.
- **The ready-made witches.** Each player found a reason to pick one in under two minutes. The ratings, Powers and Lives give each a clear job: Dana frees and hits, June finds and studies, Noor brews and leads the Unmaking.
- **The lead write-ups.** Each lead's two answers ("What and where" and "Weakness") made the Storyteller's job easy. The rule to tell the whole Weakness plainly at the last Clue stopped any "but we only have 'A. Lisk'" stall.
- **Go now, or stay.** Chapter 6's table and Chapter 14's box said it plainly, and the players understood the mark at once.
- **Life calls.** They gave a real trade: Noor took a potion over her stocktake and paid 1 Magic. Dana's night shift and the voice clinic being in the same hospital made a good scene, and the rule (one action) was clear.
- **The twist.** Keeping Grace's secret was a real moral choice with no rules attached. The players liked deciding it themselves.
- **The showdown core.** Round order, Broken, "Doom doesn't rise once Broken", the Unmaking's Difficulty and timing, and "the episode ends at once" all worked with no questions. The Chapter 7 worked example of freeing Grace at the Night Market matched what happened in Drill A.
- **The public showdown (Drill A).** "Bunch your casting into the same rounds", calling Rook to Carry Grace as soon as she was free, Sight's Surge on a study to find the Weakness mid-fight, and keeping Magic for the Unmaking all came straight from the book's advice and all mattered.
- **Guarding, taking and losing the innocent; Seen; retreat; Scars.** Every one was answered in one place, quickly (Drills C, E, H). "No Scar on a retreat" gave the players a clear reason to retreat.
- **Leaving your Power off.** In Drill B3, both witches defended with Powers off, so the round didn't raise Exposure. Once taught from Chapter 2, players used it on purpose.
- **Rule boxes and the quick reference.** Every showdown rule the table needed was on the two quick-reference pages.

## 6. How it felt

**Was losing a real risk?** Not in the episode. The threat made one attack all night, and it missed. No witch marked a single Hurt. Exposure never moved. Doom ended at 3 of 12. The coven had all 9 Clues by the end of scene 2, thanks to two Criticals in scene 1. At Lead Difficulty 1, a 4- or 5-die lead fails only 3–6% of the time, so the investigation had almost no costs. At Easy, with the Weakness known, three working attacks plus one Blasting potion (5 + 4 + 4) Break a 12-Toughness threat in round 1. This matches what the book promises for Easy ("almost none end in a Loss"; "A witch goes Down in only 2% of episodes"). It also means the first night never showed the players a cost, a Down witch or Exposure. The drills showed the risk is real once the threat comes to a public place. Exposure reached 4 of 5 in Drill A. Seen dropped a Win to a Partial in Drill E. Without the Weakness, two Unmakings failed and he fled (Drill F). One bad public round pushed the coven to retreat (Drill H).

**Were Magic, Ward and potions spent?** Partly.
- **Magic:** the witches started with 15, gained 2 from Lives and lost 1 for Noor's unanswered call. They spent 8 (a Reveal, two Blasts, the Unmaking's lead and joins, two help dice). The other 8 were lost at the end (Dana 4, June 2, Noor 2). At Difficulty 1 nobody needed to buy dice, and no Surge was used in the episode.
- **Potions:** 2 of 3 were used (Blasting and Binding, both on round-1 attacks). The Healing potion spoiled. One brewed potion was lost to the limit.
- **Ward:** there was no guardian player. The call wasn't used in the episode, but it was used well in Drill A (Carry) and Drill G (Mend).

**Was there a dominant tactic?**
- **Noor at home doing Book research plus a brew** (Wild fits both) was the strongest single action: a Clue and two potions for one action, with no Exposure.
- At Easy, **"everyone attack in round 1, with a Blasting potion"** Breaks the threat before it acts a second time.

Neither is broken. Easy is meant to be gentle, and the Book researcher's brew is capped by the potion limit. In the public drills the strongest play was simply what the book advises: bunch casting, Carry the innocent with the call, study for the Weakness. Nothing degenerate turned up.

**How long did each part take?**

| Part | Book | This table |
|---|---|---|
| Setup (steps 1–7) | 25 min | 32 min |
| Investigation | 60–80 min ("usually 3 scenes at Easy") | 51 min, 3 scenes (17, 22, 12) |
| Showdown | 30–40 min ("usually 2 rounds at Easy") | 22 min: 8 teaching the rules, then 2 rounds |
| Closing | 10–15 min | 10 min |
| **Session** | **2–3 hours** | **about 1 h 55 min** |
| Drills (if played at a table) | — | about 70 min |

**Did each player get real choices?**
- **Investigation:** yes, but thin. Every player had a lead each scene: which lead, Reveal or search, Book plus brew or a lead. Lead Difficulty 1 meant the choice of lead barely changed the odds. The "go now or stay" choice had no tension, because the Clues ran out before Doom mattered.
- **Keeping magic secret:** not in the episode. Nothing pushed Exposure, so no one weighed leaving a Power off or casting Hide. It came alive only in the public drills: bunching casting, leaving Powers off in a quiet round, and the fear of Seen at 4 of 5. Without Voice or Veil, covering up was weak (Noor's Craft 3 Hide failed in Drill I).
- **Fighting:** a little. The players chose who attacks first, which potion goes on which attack and whether to Break now or later, but at Easy the order barely mattered. In Drill A the freeing roll was a real moment: Dana's 6-die Partial, then Noor's 3-die Success. Two of the three witches have Nerve 1, so in a public fight only Dana frees well. Players noticed and liked having to plan around it.
- **Everyday life:** yes. The Life calls forced a trade (Noor skipped hers for a potion; Dana and June gave up scene 3). Grace's secret gave a real choice, and the closing gave each witch a cost from their week.

**Spotlight:** Dana had two leads (Life fitted both), a Life scene, the big potion hit and a join in the Unmaking. June had the Reveal Critical, the Grace scene and twist, her Life scene, the Blast that Broke him and a join. Noor had the crows in the lanes, the Book and two brews, the Binding Blast, and she led the Unmaking. Nobody sat out.

**Summary:** the first night ran cleanly from the book, with no blockers and five confusing points (Hush's scope, the quick reference's "leave your Power off" line, Difficulty 0, target ties, the opening). But at Easy the dice never pushed back. The table finished feeling clever rather than tested, and the public showdown, which is where this game's tension lives, only appeared in the drills.
