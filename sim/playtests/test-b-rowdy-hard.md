# Test B: rowdy table at Hard (core rules v0.4)

- **Rules tested:** `docs/CORE-RULES.md` v0.4, played strictly by the text. Where the text is silent, the ruling is logged and numbered (see section 3).
- **Date:** 2026-10-30.
- **Table:** 5 experienced players (4 witches and 1 guardian player) and a Storyteller, at Hard.
- **Brief:** they split up, take risks, use every Power, spell, potion and gift they can, push every track, and wait for the Weakness so that Doom reaches the Showdown mark.
- **Dice:** every die comes from a dice script using a computer random number generator. Each roll is logged with a label. Section 8 lists all 104 rolls in order, and the roll IDs (B001…) are cited throughout.

## 4. Drills

Each drill uses fresh dice. Unless a drill says otherwise, the setup is a public showdown at Hard with the coven from section 1.

**D1. Every witch Down.**
- **Setup:** no Weakness (Defence 3). Abel had faded home. Dana and Tess were Down (Hurt 4), Nadia was at Hurt 3, Lou at Hurt 2, and the threat at 14/20.
- **Witches act:** Nadia attacked (B040): 1 success against Difficulty 3 is a Failure, because half of 3 rounds up to 2. Its 1 Hurt cost put her Down. Lou attacked (B041): Failure, Hurt 3.
- **Threat targets:** B042 = 5 picks the most hurt witch still standing, Lou. B043 = 1 picks "whoever hurt it last", Tess, who is Down. By ruling 8, that became Lou too.
- **Lou defends twice:**
  - B044: Nerve 2 + Veil 2, plus 1 Magic each from Dana and Tess (both Down; Down witches can help): Success.
  - B045: Nadia (Down) helps, and Lou adds 1 of her own Magic: Critical.
- **Next round:** Lou attacked with no Magic left (B046): Partial, 1 damage (15/20). The cost of 1 Hurt put her Down. Every witch was Down, so by ruling 16 the episode ended at once. B047 and B048 were rolled before that ruling and were not used.
- **Result:** Loss. All four witches get a Scar. The guardian doesn't count toward "every witch Down".
- **Finding:** in a showdown every Partial or Failure costs Hurt, so a witch at 3 Hurt can end the episode with her own roll. Help from Down witches kept Lou up for one round.

**D2. Doom filling during the showdown.**
- **D2a:**
  - **Setup:** Doom 11, threat 13/20, Weakness known.
  - **Rolls:** Tess Partial, 2 damage (B049). Nadia Critical, 4 (B050). Dana Failure (B051). Lou Partial, 2 (B052). That makes 21/20.
  - **Result:** Broken during the actions, so Doom stayed at 11. The order line handled it cleanly.
- **D2b:**
  - **Setup:** Doom 11, threat 5/20. Dana was already Down. Abel was guarding Eli, who was free.
  - **Rolls:** Tess Failure (B053). Nadia Partial, 2 (B054). Lou Partial, 2 (B055). The threat reached 9/20, and Tess, Nadia and Lou each took 1 Hurt.
  - **Threat:** both target dice said "most hurt" (B056 = 4, B057 = 5), a three-way tie at 1 Hurt. The tie-break dice picked Lou (B058) and Tess (B059). Lou failed her defence (B060) and Tess failed hers (B061): 3 Hurt each, so both were Down.
  - **Then:** Exposure +1 (Powers were used). Doom reached 12: Loss. Dana, Lou and Tess get Scars.
  - **Gap:** "It escapes with the innocent", but Eli was free and guarded (issue 14).

**D3. Seen while already losing.**
- **Setup:** Exposure 3/4, Doom 11, threat 4/20, no Veil Surge.
- **Roll:** Tess Blast with Craft 2 + Flame 2 + 2 of her own Magic (B062): Success. 2 + 1 (Blast) + 1 (Weakness) = 4, so 8/20.
- **Then:** Exposure reached 4, so the coven was Seen. Doom reached 12: Loss. "A Loss stays a Loss", so the Exposure and the 3 Magic cost nothing extra.
- **Finding:** once a Loss is certain, or once the coven is Seen, public magic is free (issue 32, A5).

**D4. The guardian going Down mid-showdown.**
- **Setup:** Abel at Hurt 2/5.
- **Hold:** Abel Held with 2 helpers (B063): Critical. Hold forced attack 1 onto him; the target dice (B064, B065) were ignored. With no defined Critical extra (issue 13), the table ruled that attack 2 also had to target him.
- **Defences:** B066 Partial, so 3/5. B067 Failure, 3 Hurt, so 6, which is capped at 5: Down.
- **Result:** he fades home "for the rest of the scene", ruled as the rest of the showdown (issue 11).
- **Count:** 4 characters are left, so the threat still makes 2 attacks. If the count fell to 3 during the fight, the threat would drop to 1 attack and gain 3 Toughness (issue 9).
- **Other reading:** he is back next round, as DESIGN.md's "until the next scene" suggests.

**D5. A Broken threat flees after a failed Vanquish.**
- **Setup:** a hidden showdown, entered at half the Clues, so no Weakness: Difficulty 6 (issue 21).
- **Round 3:** Tess Broke the threat with her action. Lou led at once with the two witches who hadn't acted: 9 dice against 6 (B068), Failure (2 successes). There was no cost beyond the Magic.
- **Round 4:** all four joined, 11 dice against 6 (B069): Partial (5). It "holds on", and at the end of round 4 it flees.
- **Result:** Partial. Next time it comes back with 2 more Toughness.
- **Spent:** Lou 4 Magic, Dana 2, Nadia 2, Tess 1, and 2 of Abel's Ward. The "keep 2 Magic" advice only made sense here, as a second try (issue 22).

**D6. Retreat.**
- **Setup:** no roll exists for this. Round 5 had just begun. Eli had been lost at the end of round 1. Doom 9, threat 6/20, Tess and Dana Down.
- **Result:** the coven retreats. That's a Loss, and Tess and Dana get Scars.

- **Gap:** how Down witches leave (issue 34). Carry "can't carry the coven out of a showdown".

**D7. Shield and Hold on the same attack.**
- **Hold:** Abel Held with 2 helpers (B070): Success. Attack 1 had to target him; its die (B071) was ignored.
- **Shield:** attack 2 (B072 = 3) went to "whoever hurt it last", Tess. Abel Shielded it for 1 Ward.

- **Finding:** with Shelter on him first (as in episode round 2), the guardian takes nothing from either attack. That's a full round with no Hurt for 1 Magic and 1 Ward (issue 5).

**D8. A held innocent when the threat is Broken.**

## 6. What worked

- **The roll is fast.** The bands are clear. The +2 cap is easy to track, and keeping the Power's +2 outside it makes Powers feel strong.
- **The order line settled questions in play.** "Resolve the characters' actions, then the threat's attacks, then Exposure, then Doom" answered three questions with no ruling needed: Broken before Doom (D2a), a Veil Surge before the Exposure step, and Healing before an attack.
- **Doom and the Showdown mark make a real choice.** The coven can go in at half the Clues, or wait for the Weakness and risk a public fight with the innocent held. The round-1 free roll was the tensest moment of the night.
- **The Life call works as a nudge.** Every witch answered, because the −1 for skipping is enough. It cost 4 of the 25 investigation actions, which feels right.
- **Exposure as the cost of magic Partials in the city** made players weigh magic dice on every lead.
- **The Vanquish was the high point.** Everyone joined, the joiners' dice outside the cap felt big, and "stays until the end of the next round" told the table exactly when to try.
- **The guardian has clear jobs:** Carry the innocent out, Mend, and draw attacks with Hold and Shield. The round-1 Carry felt heroic.
- **Everything got used.** Every Power the coven had, every spell, every potion and every gift came up at least once in play (Force, Wild and Shield in drills). Magic and Ward were spent to the last point.

## 7. How it felt

- **Was losing a real risk? Not in the episode.**
  - The Shade never made an attack. Bind and a Binding potion cancelled both in round 1, and it Broke on the last action of round 2. All 4 Hurt in the fight came from the witches' own Partials.
  - There were two real risks: the round-1 free roll (4 dice against 2, about 69%), and walking into a public fight at Exposure 3 (the Veil Surge needed only a Partial on 5 dice, about 97%).
  - The drills show that losses do happen when the coven has no potion stock or no Weakness (D1, D2b, D5).
- **Were resources spent? Yes.** Magic 23 of 24 and Ward 5 of 5 were spent. 7 of 10 potions were used, and 3 Binding potions spoiled.
- **Dominant tactics:**
  1. Stockpile Blasting and Binding potions. Brewing is near-certain (a Partial still makes two), and the scene spent waiting for Doom was free brewing time.
  2. Use Veil's Surge on Hide early in a public fight. It removes Exposure for the whole showdown.
  3. Bind plus a Binding potion wipes out a round of attacks.
  4. Once the Weakness is known, a Partial does 2 damage (3 with Blast), so even a Nerve 1 witch chips in usefully.
- **Is waiting for the mark ever worth it?** Only for the extra prep scenes. Going in with half or all of the Clues keeps the innocent out of the fight. A table that hadn't been briefed would have gone in at Doom 4, as soon as it had the Weakness.
- **Simulator note:** the simulator's Hard cell for 5 players with a guardian shows Win 59% and Loss 17% (`sim/REPORT.md`). This table's potion-first play is likely above that. A potion-hoarding policy in the simulator would show by how much.
- **Time (estimate for a real table):** about 1 hour 45 minutes.
  - Setup: about 20 minutes (no Life table, and the threat was built by hand).
  - Investigation: 5 scenes, about 45 minutes.
  - Showdown: 3 rounds, about 20 minutes.
  - Rulings talk: about 15 minutes.
  - Closing: 5 minutes.
- **Roll stats:**
  - Episode: 32 rolls with a result. Critical 16%, Failure 6%.
  - All 81 rolls with a result (episode and drills): Critical 17%, Failure 17%. The drills lean towards hard spots.
